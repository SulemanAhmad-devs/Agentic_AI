For each job application, tailor the CV to the role by
matching keywords from the job description to your projects
and skills. Keep a tracker with the company name, date 
applied, contact person, and status. Follow up after one 
week if you haven't heard back. Prepare for interviews by 
reviewing your own projects, practicing explanations out loud, 
and preparing questions to ask the interviewer about the team and the stack. 
Mock interviews with a friend help a lot with nerves.
