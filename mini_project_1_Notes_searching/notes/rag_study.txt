Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a search step with a language model. 
Documents are split into chunks and embedded into vectors stored in a database such
as Chroma or FAISS. When a user asks a question, the system embeds the question, 
retrieves the most similar chunks, and passes them to the LLM as context. This lets
the model answer from your own documents and reduces made-up answers. 
Chunk size and the number of retrieved results strongly affect answer quality.
